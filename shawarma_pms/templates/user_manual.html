User Manual

Choose a system module to read its guide. This manual covers everyday workflows and the settings available to your role.

Dashboard Overview

A quick summary of store activity.

What you see

  • Gross Sales: sales before deductions.
  • Net Profit: estimated profit for the selected period.
  • Cash Outflow: recorded cash paid out.
  • Unpaid Receivables: amounts not yet paid.
  • Charts show sales trends and top-selling products.

Quick controls

  • Date filter: change the period shown.
  • Bell: view alerts and updates.
  • Sidebar: open a system page. Items depend on your access.
  • Attendance: clock in or out for your shift.

Your account

  • Your role decides which pages and actions are available.
  • Use Account & Security to change your password.
  • Sign out when finished, especially on a shared device.

Inventory

Manage menu items, ingredients, and stock records.

Products & categories

  • Product Management: view product, variant, category, price, and status.
  • Add Product / Save Product: create a menu item.
  • Category filter: narrow the list.
  • Category Management: add, edit, or activate/deactivate categories.

Ingredient Stock

  • View ingredient name, category, quantity, unit cost, and status.
  • Add Ingredient: add an ingredient to stock.
  • Adjust: record stock coming in or going out. Enter the amount and reason.
  • Use the category filter to find ingredients.

Recipes & movements

  • Recipe Management: select a product, then add ingredients and required amounts.
  • Remove: delete an ingredient from a recipe.
  • Stock Movements: review quantity changes, balance, reason, and user.
  • Type filter: show selected movement types.
  • Export Target + Export PDF/CSV: choose rows and download them.

Expiration & spoilage

  • Report Spoilage: choose ingredient and loss type, enter quantity and reason, then submit.
  • The list shows when, what, how much, why, and who reported the loss.
  • Check Stock & Spoilage Reports for loss summaries.

Checkout

Create an order, take payment, and track preparation.

New Sales buttons

  • Search / category buttons: find menu items.
  • Dine-in / Take-out: set the order type.
  • + / −: change item quantity.
  • Add note: send an instruction with an item.
  • Remove: delete an item from the cart.
  • Discount / Apply: select an eligible promotion or voucher.
  • Void: cancel the order after confirmation.
  • Pay now: continue to payment.

Payment buttons

  • +50 / +100 / +500 / +1000: add cash to the amount field.
  • Exact amount: enter the amount due. Clear: empty the field.
  • Select the shown e-wallet provider when using e-wallet payment.
  • Confirm payment: record the payment. Wait for confirmation.
  • Reprint receipt: print another copy. New order: start again.

Orders & kitchen

  • Kitchen Orders: view tickets and update preparation status.
  • Order List: view orders, totals, payment, and status.
  • Transaction History: review order status changes.
  • Receipts: find a sale and open or print its receipt.

Billing & Payment

Collect payment and confirm the receipt.

Complete a payment

  • Check the order items, discount, charges, and total.
  • Select the payment method and enter the amount or reference.
  • For cash, check the received amount and change.
  • Select Confirm payment once. Wait for success confirmation.
  • Use Reprint receipt if another copy is needed.

Receipts & charges

  • Receipts show the sale and payment details.
  • Store name, currency, VAT, service charge, and footer come from General Settings.
  • If payment looks wrong, check the order and receipt before retrying.

Reports & Analytics

Review sales, products, stock, and demand.

Sales & products

  • Sales Reports: order, date, items, payment method, total, and cashier.
  • Product Sales: product, category, quantity sold, price, and revenue.
  • Set the date or available filters before reviewing results.

Forecast & inventory

  • Sales Forecast: past performance, busy hours, product trends, and expected sales.
  • Inventory Reports: stock quantity, minimum level, and stock status.
  • Stock & Spoilage: lost items, amount, reason, reporter, and estimated cost.

Export a report

  • Choose Export Target to select which rows to include.
  • Export PDF: download a print-friendly file.
  • Export CSV: download data for a spreadsheet.
  • Reports use recorded system data. Correct errors in the source page.

User Management

Manage employee accounts, permissions, attendance, and shifts.

Employee List

  • View employee ID, name, username, role, and status.
  • Add Employee: enter the requested employee and account details, then save.
  • Row actions can activate/deactivate or remove an account. Check before confirming.

User Roles & Access

  • Select an employee and review their permission checkboxes.
  • Select All Permissions: grant every listed permission. Clear Permissions: remove all checks.
  • Save the intended access for the employee's job.
  • Temporary access: choose employee, module, and duration, then submit. Use Revoke to end it early.

Attendance & schedules

  • Attendance: clock in/out; history shows employee, date, times, and status.
  • Work Schedules: choose employee, date, shift, and role, then save.
  • Use period and employee filters to review the schedule.
  • Use a row action to change or remove a shift when available.

Settings & Maintenance

Update store preferences, account security, and system backups.

General Settings

  • Store Profile: store name, branch, address, contact, and email.
  • Hours & Charges: opening/closing time, VAT, and service charge.
  • Receipt: currency, VAT display, and footer.
  • Operations: stock blocking, kitchen target time, and manager PIN.
  • Save Changes: save your updates. Fix any fields named in an error message.

Account & Activity

  • Account & Security: enter current password and new password twice; Save Changes updates it.
  • Eye buttons show or hide typed passwords.
  • Activity Logs: search recorded actions by user, action, or description.

Backup & Maintenance

  • Run Diagnostics: check system health.
  • Create Backup: set a password and download an encrypted backup.
  • Restore Backup: select a backup and enter its password. This replaces current data.
  • Wipe Data: permanently removes system records. Use only when intended.
